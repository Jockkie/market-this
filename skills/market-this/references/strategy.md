# Strategy, distribution and the 90-day plan

## First 10, 100, 1,000

- **First 10:** almost always direct. Write the list of 30 people or companies (by role or segment, not personal data in the dossier) who fit the ICP and could buy now. Reach out personally. Learn from every conversation.
- **First 100:** the one channel that worked for the first ten, made repeatable, plus referral from the first ten.
- **First 1,000:** a second channel, partnerships, and a system (content engine, ads, affiliates) once the message is proven.

## The 90-day plan

Use `templates/gtm-plan.md`. Three blocks of four weeks plus a review:

| Block | Weeks | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Fix | 1 to 4 | Fix the weakest pillar, first direct outreach, set up measurement |
| Test | 5 to 8 | Two channel tests, first proof collected, price or offer test |
| Double down | 9 to 12 | Keep the winner, kill the loser, build the weekly rhythm into a routine |
| Review | 13 | Re-score the pillars, next weakest pillar |

## Weekly rhythm

Each week has fixed slots in the user's own capacity, for example:
- one outreach block (to someone who can pay; content does not count for this)
- one creation block (the main piece for the chosen channel)
- one distribution block (repurpose, share, reply, partners)
- fifteen minutes of measurement (the weekly numbers below)

Put the slots in the calendar if the platform allows it, with the user's consent, respecting their work days.

## Content calendar

Use `templates/content-calendar.csv`. One pillar topic per month, one main piece per week, repurposed per channel according to the live guidelines. Every piece has a goal (reach, trust, conversion) and a call to action.

## Metrics (AARRR)

| Step | Metric | Example |
|------|--------|---------|
| Acquisition | Reach and visitors per channel | Visitors, impressions, list sign-ups |
| Activation | First meaningful action | Inquiry, trial start, call booked |
| Revenue | Paying customers, average order | Sales per week, revenue |
| Retention | Repeat or renewal | Repeat purchase rate |
| Referral | New customers from existing ones | Referred customers per month |

Choose one north-star metric for the 90 days, usually tied to revenue. Record the baseline in week 0.

## Launch

For a launch: a waiting list or pre-sale first, a launch date, a launch week with daily touch points, partners lined up, proof ready, and a follow-up sequence. Simulate the launch message first if MiroFish is available.
