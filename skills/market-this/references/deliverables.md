# Deliverables

At the end of Phase 8 the user receives, in their language:

1. **GTM diagnosis:** ten pillar scores with evidence, the stage, the weakest pillar and why.
2. **Positioning and message house:** from `templates/positioning.md`.
3. **Channel plan:** motion, Bullseye result, two channel tests with budget and threshold, the live guidelines found per channel with date and source.
4. **90-day plan:** from `templates/gtm-plan.md`, with first customers list (roles or segments), weekly rhythm, launch moments.
5. **Competitor matrix and pricing result** (if those pillars were worked on).
6. **Content calendar** (printed in the reply as a table; also as `content-calendar.csv` only if you can actually write files): `templates/content-calendar.csv` filled for the first four weeks.
7. **Brand brief:** from `templates/brand-brief.md`.
8. **Baseline and metrics:** north-star metric, AARRR baseline, review date.
9. **Dossier:** JSON plus a readable Markdown version.
10. **Dashboard (optional):** `scripts/dashboard.py` output, one HTML file.

Deliver files where the platform supports it, otherwise as clear blocks in the conversation. Keep each document short enough to actually use.
