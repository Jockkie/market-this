# Interview: ten topics and Gate 1

Ask one question at a time. Start broad, then narrow down to numbers and real words. Mirror back after each topic in one or two sentences. If a Consult Anything dossier exists, skip what it already answers and confirm it instead.

## The ten topics

| # | Topic | Core questions | Done when |
|---|-------|----------------|-----------|
| 1 | Offer | What exactly do you sell? What does the customer get, in what form, how fast? What does it cost? | The offer is described in one sentence a stranger understands, with a price or price range. |
| 2 | Goal | What should be different in 90 days? How many customers, what revenue, what margin? | One measurable goal with a number and a date. |
| 3 | Current customers | Who bought so far? What do the best customers have in common? Who should never have bought? | At least one concrete best-fit segment, or "no customers yet" stated. |
| 4 | Problem and trigger | What problem made them look? What happened just before they bought? What did they try before? | The trigger event and the old solution are known, ideally in the customer's own words. |
| 5 | Alternatives | What would the customer do if you did not exist? Who or what do you lose deals to? | At least two alternatives named (competitors, doing nothing, doing it themselves, hiring someone). |
| 6 | Why you | Why do customers pick you? What can you do that the alternatives cannot? Can you prove it? | At least one unique attribute with proof, or "unclear" stated honestly. |
| 7 | Current marketing | Where do customers come from today? Which channels did you try, for how long, with what result? | Each tried channel has a duration and a rough result. |
| 8 | Funnel numbers | Visitors or contacts per month, leads, conversations, sales, average order, repeat rate. | The main funnel steps have a number or range, labeled. |
| 9 | Resources | Hours per week for marketing, budget per month, skills in the team, assets (list, network, content, brand). | Hours and budget are known. |
| 10 | Constraints and values | What will you never do in marketing? Legal or sector rules? Seasonality? | Hard no's and sector rules are recorded. |

## Real customer words

Ask for: reviews, emails, chat messages, sales call notes, the words customers use when they recommend you. Collect phrases verbatim in the dossier under `voice_of_customer`. If there are none, suggest five short customer conversations with these questions:
1. What was going on when you started looking for this?
2. What did you try before?
3. What almost stopped you from buying?
4. What would you tell a friend about it?
5. What would you miss if it disappeared?

## When numbers are missing

Offer three routes and let the user choose:
1. **Range estimate:** "Roughly how many inquiries per month: under 5, 5 to 20, 20 to 50, more?"
2. **Two-week measurement:** count visitors, inquiries, conversations and sales per channel in a simple sheet.
3. **Share data:** analytics export, CRM export, shop dashboard screenshot. Anonymize first (see `privacy.md`).

## Optional public research

With consent, and only public business information: their website, public reviews, competitor sites and pricing pages, search demand for their category. Via Monid if connected (quote and cap first, see `tools.md`), otherwise by web search or by the user pasting it in.

## Gate 1

Show the checklist with each topic marked done or open, then a summary of eight to twelve lines. Ask: "Does this match? Anything missing or wrong?" Continue only after the user confirms.
